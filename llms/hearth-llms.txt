# Hearth

> Hearth is a business-management platform for residential home-improvement contractors and trades professionals — customer financing (18+ lender marketplace, $0 dealer fees), quotes and estimates, digital contracts and e-signature, invoicing and payments, client management, scheduling, and Harper, a 24/7 AI receptionist. Serves 16+ contracting verticals (roofing, HVAC, plumbing, landscaping, fencing, flooring). Backed by 8vc. No public developer API is published at this time.
